01 — BRAND ESSENCE

Chess is intellectual
combat dressed in silk.

The Classical Chess Collegium is not a kids' chess app. It is not a cute learning tool. It is a cognitive development platform that treats chess with the reverence it deserves — as a 1,500-year-old discipline that builds minds. Our brand reflects that gravity. Dark. Gold. Precise. Unapologetic.

Luxurious
Not expensive. Elevated. Every touchpoint feels considered and refined.
Intellectual
We respect the intelligence of our audience. We never dumb it down.
Confident
We don't explain ourselves. We present and let the work speak.
BRAND POSITION
We are the Equinox of scholastic chess — where cognitive performance meets design discipline. Not the cheapest. Not the most colorful. The most serious.

ANATOMY OF THE MARK

A 2×2 grid of squares — two filled, two outlined — arranged on a diagonal. The filled squares (top-left, bottom-right) represent presence. The outlined squares represent potential. Together they echo the 64-square geometry of the chessboard distilled to its purest expression.

The diagonal rhythm mirrors the bishop's path. The grid mirrors the board. The restraint mirrors the brand. Four squares. That's all we need.

03 — COLOR SYSTEM

Black and gold.
Nothing else needed.

Our palette is deliberately narrow. Restraint is the point. Gold only works because we use it sparingly — it's the accent, never the background. The darkness makes the gold feel earned.

COLOR RATIO
65% Obsidian · 20% Charcoal · 10% Royal Gold · 5% Parchment. Gold is the reward for restraint.

05 — VOICE & TONE

Speak like you
belong at the table.

Confident, not arrogant
"We built the cognitive layer that chess has been missing."
"We're the best chess platform ever created!!!"
Precise, not cold
"90-day longitudinal data across 5 cognitive domains."
"Lots of great data about how your kid is doing."
Ambitious, not desperate
"What PlayVS did for esports, we do for chess."
"Please try our app, it's really good we promise."
Human, not corporate
"Chess changed Sarah's life. We're building the platform she needed as a kid."
"Our solution leverages synergies in the EdTech space."
THE RULE
Write like a chess grandmaster speaks — measured, deliberate, and never more words than the position requires.
06 — IMAGERY DIRECTION

Photography &
visual language.

Macro Chess
Tight crops on pieces. Shallow depth of field. Dramatic side lighting. The texture of wood, the weight of marble, the gleam of brass. Chess pieces as objects of beauty.
Hands & Boards
A child's hand moving a piece. A mentor observing. Focus on the moment of decision. Never staged smiles — capture concentration, intensity, the face of thinking.
Dark & Moody
Low-key lighting. Deep shadows. Single light source preferred. Think editorial photography, not stock photos. If it could be in Kinfolk magazine, it belongs here.
Architecture of the Board
Overhead shots of board geometry. The 64 squares as a landscape. Patterns and lines. The board is architecture, not decoration.
NEVER
Clip art. Cartoon chess pieces. Stock photos of kids high-fiving. Rainbow gradients. Flat illustrations. Anything that makes chess look like a daycare activity.
